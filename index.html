Dual-Sim Interlocked Physical-Generative World Simulator
for Robotic Manipulation

Physics Simulator governs the robot. Generative Simulator completes the world.

Abstract

Action-conditioned video world models, i.e., world simulators, predict future videos of a robot executing given actions, offering a photorealistic environment for robotic manipulation. However, three limitations remain in existing methods: (1) generated robot motion gradually drifts from the given actions over long horizons; (2) each new embodiment requires a separately fine-tuned model; and (3) the proprioceptive state needed for policy training and evaluation is missing or inaccurate. In contrast, a physics simulator explicitly computes robot kinematics and states for any specified embodiment, naturally compensating for these limitations.

To this end, we present Dual-Sim, a world simulator that interlocks a physics simulator with a generative simulator, combining the former's kinematic fidelity with the latter's visual realism. Specifically, Dual-Sim casts world prediction as a conditional transport from simulated kinematics to the target video and proprioceptive state. This transport is learned by a video-state Mixture-of-Transformers, whose video and state experts exchange features along a shared flow to predict both modalities jointly.

We further propose kinematic seeding, which replaces the Gaussian flow source with a noisy inversion of the simulated kinematics, so that the generative simulator refines the robot and completes the scene rather than imagining the future from scratch. Dual-Sim surpasses existing methods in long-horizon action following, zero-shot cross-embodiment generalization, and proprioceptive state accuracy. Closed-loop experiments further demonstrate its utility for real-world policy evaluation and training data generation.

Dual-Sim Framework

Dual-Sim interlocks a physics simulator with a generative simulator, casting world prediction as a conditional transport from simulated kinematics to future video and proprioceptive state via a shared Video-State Mixture-of-Transformers.

01

Physics Simulation

Given the current state q0 and action sequence A, the physics simulator executes robot kinematics against an empty background to produce the multi-view sim-video Ṽ and exact proprioceptive sim-state q̃.

02

Kinematic Seeding

Rather than denoising from pure Gaussian noise, kinematic seeding builds a hybrid flow source from a noisy inversion of the simulated kinematics, providing an editable prior for the robot while leaving the surrounding scene unconstrained.

03

Video-State Mixture-of-Transformers

The video and state experts maintain separate weights and exchange features via bidirectional cross-attention along a shared conditional flow, jointly refining robot appearance, completing the scene, and adapting joint states under contact.

Quantitative Results

Visual Quality

Achieves 21.56 dB PSNR and 74.90 SSIM on 300-frame RichInteraction rollouts, preserving sharp scene appearance and embodiment fidelity.

Action Consistency

Leads across Trajectory nDTW (44.99), HSD (33.65), and Dynamics (16.84), mitigating long-horizon motion drift and deformation.

Cross-Embodiment

A single model generalizes zero-shot to the unseen Franka-Panda robot, reaching 60.23 Emb. SSIM and 2.2× Trajectory Accuracy.

Action-Conditioned Video Generation

Evaluated on 100 episodes from AGIBOT WORLD 2026 RichInteraction (300-frame rollouts). Dual-Sim leads in overall EWMScore, Action Consistency, and Visual Quality.
